---
name: Aetherfall homebrew scope
description: Campaign ownership and import behavior for Aetherfall-specific monsters.
---

Aetherfall monsters are campaign-owned homebrew content and must not appear in the shared bestiary. New homebrew monster imports append to the campaign's existing collection; replacement happens only through an explicit clear operation.

**Why:** Aetherfall creatures are setting-specific, and adding later creations must not destroy previously entered campaign content.

**How to apply:** Store Aetherfall-only creatures under the Aetherfall campaign. Treat normal homebrew imports as additive and reserve destructive removal for a clearly labeled, confirmed clear action.

For hybrid modules, keep their nonstandard combat mechanics rather than silently replacing them with ordinary fifth-edition rules. In particular, Until First Light uses Zombicide kill thresholds for several zombies, not conventional HP.

**Why:** The user wants the module's usable statblocks in the initiative bestiary, not newly invented fifth-edition conversions. A tracker convenience counter must not be presented as printed HP.

**How to apply:** Label manual counters explicitly and preserve the source's kill conditions and activation rules. Do not imply that the initiative tracker automatically enforces the module's separate zombie phase, same-turn hit thresholds, or special instant-kill effects.
