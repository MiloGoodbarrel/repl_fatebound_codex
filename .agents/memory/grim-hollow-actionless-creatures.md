---
name: Grim Hollow actionless creatures
description: Source-fidelity exception for creatures with no printed action section.
---

Spythronar Sac and Eye Crawler intentionally have no action rows. The former has only triggered traits, while the latter explicitly has no effective attacks.

**Why:** Requiring at least one imported action for every Grim Hollow creature would fabricate content and incorrectly flag these two source-accurate exceptions.

**How to apply:** Completeness checks should cover all printed action sections and allow these two creatures to have zero action rows.
