---
name: Roll Tables content model
description: Durable content and persistence boundaries for the Roll Tables workspace.
---

Stock roll-table content must be original and system-neutral rather than copied from published game tables. Custom tables belong to one campaign and use either contiguous, non-overlapping numeric ranges or positive weighted chances.

**Why:** This keeps the built-in catalog broadly usable without reproducing copyrighted material, while making custom results predictable and safely persistent for each campaign.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints when adding stock content, validation, imports, or future table mechanics. Treat nested tables, macros, conditional rules, sharing, CSV exchange, and automatic cross-tool application as separate future scope.
