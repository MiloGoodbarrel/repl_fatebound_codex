Here is the breakdown of the official rules (Rules as Written / RAW):

1. Healing Resets Everything
The moment a character at 0 HP receives any amount of healing (even just 1 HP from a Healing Word or a potion):

Consciousness: They immediately regain consciousness.

Reset: Their current count of death save successes and failures are both reset to zero.

State: They are no longer "dying" or "unstable."

Prone: They are still on the ground (prone) and must spend half their movement to stand up on their turn.

2. Stabilization vs. Healing
If you don't have healing magic, you can Stabilize a creature (using a DC 10 Medicine check or a Healer's Kit):

Successes/Failures: These also reset to zero the moment the creature becomes stable.

Consciousness: The creature remains unconscious at 0 HP.

Waking Up: A stable creature regains 1 HP naturally after 1d4 hours, at which point they wake up.

Taking Damage: If a stable creature takes damage, they stop being stable and must start making death saves again from scratch.

3. The "Nat 20" Rule
If a player rolls a Natural 20 on a death saving throw:

They immediately regain 1 HP.

Because they now have 1 HP, they are conscious and their death saves reset to zero instantly. They can take the rest of their turn normally.

4. Important Combat Penalties
No Negative HP: There is no "-5 HP" in 5e. If you have 2 HP and take 10 damage, you just go to 0 (unless the leftover damage equals your max HP, which results in instant death).

Damage at 0 HP: If a character is at 0 HP and takes damage, it is an automatic death save failure. If the attacker is within 5 feet, it’s a critical hit, which counts as two failures.

Common Homebrew (The "Exhaustion" Rule)
Because the "reset" rule makes it very easy for players to pop up and down repeatedly without consequence, many DMs use this popular house rule:

"Every time you drop to 0 HP and are brought back up, you gain one level of Exhaustion."

This adds a lingering penalty to hitting 0 HP, making the "yo-yo" strategy much more dangerous over a long adventuring day.

For your Replit app: You should ensure that the "Heal" function includes a line of code that sets death_save_successes = 0 and death_save_failures = 0 whenever the target's HP goes above zero.
